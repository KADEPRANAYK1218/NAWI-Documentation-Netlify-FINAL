SIH26035 — References

Legal Metrology, NAWI & OIML R 76

Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India — Legal Metrology
Official Government of India resource covering the Legal Metrology framework, including the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and related rules and regulatory information applicable to weighing and measuring instruments.
https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act

Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011 — Government of India
Official regulatory framework prescribing requirements and procedures under the Legal Metrology Act, including provisions relevant to weighing and measuring instruments, verification, stamping, and compliance.

OIML Recommendation R 76 — Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments (NAWI)
International legal-metrology recommendation specifying metrological and technical requirements, test procedures, and associated requirements for non-automatic weighing instruments. It provides the technical basis for evaluating NAWI performance and preparing structured test documentation.

Relevance to SIH26035

These references support the technical and regulatory concepts used in the SIH26035: Development of a Software Program/Application for Generation of Test Reports for Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments (NAWI) as per OIML Recommendation R 76 problem statement, including:

Non-automatic weighing instrument (NAWI) testing

OIML R 76-based test procedures and requirements

Legal Metrology compliance in India

Verification and stamping of weighing instruments

Recording of test observations and measurement results

Automated calculation and validation of test parameters

Generation of standardized test reports

Traceability of instrument, test, and inspector information

Digital documentation and report management

Reduction of manual data-entry and calculation errors

Compliance-oriented software workflows

Reference Dataset / Regulatory Source

The Department of Consumer Affairs, Government of India — Legal Metrology resource can be used as an official reference source for the Indian legal-metrology framework:

https://consumeraffairs.gov.in/pages/legal-metrology-act

The Legal Metrology (General) Rules, 2011 should be used alongside OIML R 76 to ensure that the application reflects the applicable Indian regulatory requirements in addition to the relevant international recommendation.

Scope Note

The proposed SIH26035 application is a software prototype/concept for assisting with the generation of test reports for Non-Automatic Weighing Instruments. The references establish the applicable legal-metrology and technical context; they do not imply that the application itself is an official Government of India, Department of Consumer Affairs, Legal Metrology Department, or OIML system.
